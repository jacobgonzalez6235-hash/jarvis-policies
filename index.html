Bedtime Butler

Bedtime Butler is a YouTube channel (@BedtimeButler) of calm, narrated science videos to fall asleep to, run by Jacob Gonzalez.

The "Bedtime Butler" app is a private tool Jacob uses to manage his own channel: uploading his videos, editing their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reading his own channel analytics through the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and accesses no one else's account.
