Privacy Policy

Jarvis Assistant is a personal AI assistant application operated solely by Jacob Gonzalez, for his own personal use.

What data is collected

Jarvis Assistant sends SMS text messages -- appointment reminders, schedule/calendar notifications, and status alerts (e.g. trading price alerts) -- to the mobile phone number of its sole user and creator, Jacob Gonzalez. No data is collected from any other individual, and no SMS opt-in data or personal information is collected from third parties.

How data is used

The phone number on file is used exclusively to deliver the automated notifications described above to its owner. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
