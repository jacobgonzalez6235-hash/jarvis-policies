Bedtime Butler Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

The Bedtime Butler app is a private tool operated by Jacob Gonzalez to manage his own YouTube channel, @BedtimeButler. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app accesses the Bedtime Butler channel's videos and their details (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and privacy settings) and the channel's YouTube Analytics reports (for example views, watch time, impressions and traffic sources). It does not access any other person's account or data.

How it uses the data

The data is used only to upload and edit the channel's own videos and to review the channel's own performance. It is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. Analytics numbers may be saved in the owner's private notes. Nothing is shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page, which deletes the app's access to the account.
